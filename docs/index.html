Over The Bar Calisthenics Group

Over The Bar Calisthenics Group provides general and tailored programs, specialized equipment and coaching as well as a dedicated calisthenic gym to help our clients become elite level athletes. We also offer free assessments and tours to our potential members. Our online store sells a variety of equipment such as rings, parallettes and dip bars. Located in Hull, just across the street from Altitude Gym, our gym has been training athletes since 2016. Our goal is to turn anybody who wants to become an athlete into an elite one.
